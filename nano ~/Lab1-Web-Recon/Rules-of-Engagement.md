Authorised Scope
----------------
Testing is restricted to the locally installed DVWA and OWASP
Mutillidae II applications within the assigned isolated training
environment.

In scope:
- DVWA at 127.0.0.1:8080
- Mutillidae II at 127.0.0.1:80
- Normal HTTP inspection
- Passive analysis
- Application navigation
- Low-rate content discovery
- Manual validation

Out of scope:
- Public/Internet systems
- Office or hospital systems
- Other hosts not assigned to this lab
- Third-party systems
- Denial-of-service testing
- High-rate scanning

Stop conditions:
- Unexpected network exposure
- Traffic reaching an unrelated host
- Application instability
- Evidence that testing has left the authorised environment
